Request account deletion outside the app

To request deletion of your account and associated data, email abdelaziz.mas3oud@gmail.com from your account email with the subject "EnjazPro account deletion request" and your business name. Do not send passwords, verification codes, or client records. We verify account ownership and explain business-owner options before deletion; submitting a request does not by itself delete a shared business. The deletion and backup details below apply.

Retention and deletion

You can delete your account from Account management. Owners can either transfer business ownership to an eligible active member and delete their account, or permanently delete their account and business without a transfer. With transfer, business, client, booking and payment records remain with the new owner. With full deletion, the business and its clients, bookings, services, payments, expenses, receipts, settings and logs are deleted; all members lose access to that business but their accounts are not deleted. In every case, the sign-in account, identity data, memberships and account-linked audit entries are deleted, and access to other businesses is removed without deleting those businesses. We do not keep a personal archive of deleted accounts for operational or marketing purposes. Copies previously exported or shared outside the app remain under their holders’ control. Provider backups may persist until the applicable retention cycle expires; they are not an archive available for operational use.

Platform administration and backups

Authorized platform administrators can access business data to manage the service, permissions, support and recovery. Administrative changes are logged. When scheduled backups are enabled, operational business data is copied daily, weekly or monthly to private Supabase storage according to the business agreement, with retention of 30, 60, 90, 180 or 365 days from creation; manual and pre-restore safety copies may also be created. The same retention applies to manual and safety copies. Shortening retention can expire existing copies for deletion in the next cleanup. Copies exclude passwords and local device files. Restoring a backup does not recreate deleted sign-in accounts or old permission grants. Deleting a business immediately blocks access to its backups and queues their files for the next cleanup cycle. Backups are not used for marketing. Provider-managed backups follow the provider’s retention cycle.
